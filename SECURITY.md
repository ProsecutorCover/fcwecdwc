## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Pitch Modulation** — Preserve vibrato by adjusting pitch center without flattening modulation
- **Formant for Gender** — Shift formants +100 cents (masculine) or -100 cents (feminine) without pitch change
- **Subtle Correction** — Correct pitch 50-80% (not 100%) for natural-sounding vocals
- **Timing Before Pitch** — Fix timing first, then pitch—correcting pitch on badly-timed vocals wastes time
- **Use ARA** — Studio One/Logic/Cubase ARA integration eliminates export/import workflow
